I want to share something essential about the nature of meditation. We are wired in such a way that, under the pressure of external stressors, our mind, awareness and eventually our body can “break” at any unpredictable moment. But if we’ve had even a small taste of meditation, we’ll remember, even in the most difficult times, that it can reconnect us with the meaning of life.
<br/>
Meditation reawakens life from within. Through it, we suddenly become aware of our essence, our inner core, our roots. It helps us protect the voice of the heart from being drowned out by the noise of other people’s opinions.
Just think about it: If a fertilised egg is broken by force from the outside, life ends. But if it is broken from within, life begins.
<br/>
Yoga and meditation have helped me through some of the most difficult moments in my life. Through them, I have:
– avoided death
– faced radical change without fear
– resolved a situation I thought was unsolvable
<br/>
Meditation does not shield us from life’s challenges. But it can serve as the oxygen mask we must first put on ourselves, so that we can then help others from a place of presence and strength.
